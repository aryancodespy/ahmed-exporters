WORKFLOW / FROM BRIEF TO DISPATCH

A clear process from first conversation to export.

Every requirement is different. A structured sourcing workflow helps buyers understand decisions, responsibilities and the information needed before an order progresses.

01THE SEVEN STAGES
01

Share the brief

Tell us the product category, intended market, approximate quantities and key specifications.

02

Assess the sourcing route

We review whether existing supply or partner-led production best fits the requirement.

03

Agree on samples and terms

Confirm materials, reference samples, quality expectations, pricing approach and commercial terms.

04

Coordinate fulfilment

We follow up with the relevant supplier or production partner against the agreed order.

05

Review and prepare

Quality, packing and readiness checks are coordinated according to the agreed scope.

06

Arrange documentation

Required export-side documentation and shipment arrangements are coordinated.

07

Dispatch and follow up

Shipment information and relevant order updates are communicated to the buyer.

02WHAT TO INCLUDE

A better brief.
A better start.

Providing key details upfront helps us assess sourcing possibilities without unnecessary back-and-forth.

  • Product category and reference images or drawings
  • Materials, dimensions, branding and finish requirements
  • Estimated quantities and target timeframe
  • Destination country and preferred delivery arrangements
  • Relevant inspection, packaging or documentation needs

Available production options, payment terms, shipment arrangements and lead times are confirmed on a case-by-case basis.
