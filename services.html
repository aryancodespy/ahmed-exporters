CAPABILITIES / SERVICES

A connected approach to international sourcing.

From finished-product sourcing to partner-led production, Ahmed Exporters helps international buyers organise the practical stages of sourcing and exporting leather goods from Bangladesh.

01OUR CORE CAPABILITIES
01

Merchant exporting

We help source commercially suitable finished goods and coordinate the supply arrangements required for export orders.

  • Supplier-led finished product sourcing
  • Order and specification alignment
  • Export-side coordination
02

International sourcing

We work from the buyer's sourcing brief to identify relevant supply routes through third-party suppliers and manufacturing partners.

  • Product and supplier exploration
  • Sample or reference discussions
  • Buyer requirement coordination
03

Third-party manufacturing coordination

For specification-based orders, we can help coordinate with independent production partners rather than claiming in-house manufacturing.

  • Sample-based product discussions
  • Partner and buyer communication
  • Production follow-up within agreed scope
02EXPORT SUPPORT

03WHO WE WORK WITH

Designed for
business buyers.

Our services are intended for importers, wholesalers, retail sourcing teams, distributors and businesses developing product lines with third-party manufacturers.

Each enquiry is assessed by category, order scope, specifications and commercial feasibility. We do not publish blanket minimum-order or delivery promises that may not apply to every supplier.
